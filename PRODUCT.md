# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Donors, supporters, volunteers, families and partner organisations who want to understand Saarah's Fund's work, verify its impact and support a current appeal with confidence.

## Product Purpose

Tell the charity's story clearly, show accurate and appropriately dated evidence of its work, and make it straightforward for visitors to donate, share an appeal or contact the charity.

## Brand Personality

Compassionate, hopeful and trustworthy. The site should feel personal and welcoming while treating impact claims and donation choices with care.

## Anti-references

Avoid corporate fundraising language, exaggerated outcomes, high-pressure donation patterns, generic charity templates and visual changes that obscure the established identity or the evidence behind impact figures.

## Design Principles

- Lead with human impact while keeping every claim precise and attributable.
- Make giving and contacting the charity simple, legible and trustworthy.
- Preserve the established visual identity during maintenance work.
- Use imagery and dated project detail to show the work rather than overstate it.
- Keep choices focused without hiding meaningful context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic controls, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support and responsive layouts. Donation and contact options must remain usable without relying on third-party script interactions.
